## 🎯 QUICK TEST (5 Minutes)

### **Test Job Report:**
```
1. Open http://localhost:3000
2. Click Dashboard icon (📊)
3. Click "Photo Job Reports" card
4. Fill in:
   - Address: "123 Test Street"
   - Customer: "Test Customer"
   - Description: "Test leak repair"
5. Click [Next]
6. Click [Upload from Gallery]
7. Select any 2-3 photos from your computer
8. Click [Next: Document Work]
9. Add text: "Replaced valve, tested 10 minutes"
10. Labor: 2 hours × £45 = £90
11. Click [Next: After Photos]
12. Upload 2 more photos
13. Click [Next: Review & Sign]
14. Click [Sign & Submit to Blockchain]
15. ✅ DONE! Job report created!
```
