## 📞 When to Hire Attorneys

**Hire Patent Attorney When:**
- You have $50K+ budget
- You get first 100 paying customers (product-market fit proven)
- A competitor launches similar product (defensive filing)
- A VC investor requires it (some do for due diligence)

**Hire Business Attorney When:**
- You sign first B2B contract >$50K
- You hire first employee
- You raise outside investment
- Customer asks for enterprise contract/SLA

**Don't Hire Attorney For:**
- Basic copyright (automatic)
- Trade secrets (just keep confidential)
- Open source license (use standard licenses)
