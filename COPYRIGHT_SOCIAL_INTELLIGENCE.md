### Novel Features & Claims

#### Claim 1: Core Innovation
A method for detecting human vulnerability conditions through non-intrusive analysis of water consumption patterns, comprising:
- Continuous monitoring of water usage data from IoT sensors
- Pattern recognition algorithms identifying behavioral anomalies
- Multi-dimensional risk scoring across health, financial, isolation, and safety domains
- Automated alert generation with context-aware intervention recommendations
- Privacy-preserving analytics without exposure of personal identifying information

#### Claim 2: Guardian Angel System
An automated emergency detection and response system for residential properties, comprising:
- Real-time monitoring for prolonged inactivity (18+ hours)
- Automatic escalation protocols based on urgency levels
- Integration with emergency contact networks and housing association systems
- Blockchain-based audit trails for accountability
- Machine learning models trained on vulnerability indicators

#### Claim 3: Social Impact Quantification
A system for measuring and reporting social value generated through early intervention, comprising:
- Lives saved estimation algorithms
- NHS cost savings calculations
- Eviction prevention metrics
- Financial crisis averted tracking
- Regulatory compliance reporting (Ofwat C-MeX, ODI rewards)
- ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) impact scoring

#### Claim 4: Water Usage as Proxy Metric
The novel application of residential water consumption patterns as a proxy indicator for:
- Health emergencies (falls, medical events)
- Financial hardship
- Mental health concerns
- Social isolation
- Child welfare issues
- Domestic disturbances

#### Claim 5: Privacy-Preserving Vulnerability Detection
A privacy-first architecture for vulnerability detection comprising:
- Anonymous pattern analysis without personal data exposure
- GDPR-compliant data processing
- Blockchain audit trails for transparency
- Opt-in consent mechanisms
- Right to be forgotten implementation

### Prior Art Analysis

**No Known Prior Art:**
- Extensive search reveals no existing systems using water consumption data for vulnerability detection
- No competitors combine IoT water monitoring with social care applications
- Novel cross-industry application (water utility + social services)
- Unique privacy-preserving architecture using blockchain audit trails

**Differentiation from Existing Systems:**
- Traditional telecare: Requires wearables or button presses (intrusive)
- Smart home monitoring: Requires cameras/sensors in living space (privacy concerns)
