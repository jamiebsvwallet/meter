## Risk Management

### Potential Risks & Mitigations

**Risk 1: False Positives**
- **Mitigation:** Multi-factor analysis, confidence scoring, human review for high urgency
- **Threshold:** <5% false positive rate acceptable

**Risk 2: Privacy Concerns**
- **Mitigation:** Anonymized data, explicit consent, transparent algorithms, GDPR compliance
- **Control:** Privacy Impact Assessment, independent audit

**Risk 3: Alert Fatigue**
- **Mitigation:** Smart thresholding, urgency categorization, configurable alerts
- **Control:** Feedback loops, continuous tuning

**Risk 4: Liability Issues**
- **Mitigation:** Clear terms of service, not a medical device, complementary tool
- **Control:** Insurance, legal review, industry standards

**Risk 5: System Downtime**
- **Mitigation:** Redundancy, failover, 24/7 monitoring, backup alerting
- **Control:** SLA guarantees, incident response plan
